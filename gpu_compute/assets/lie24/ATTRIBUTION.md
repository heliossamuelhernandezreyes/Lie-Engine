# LIE-24 facial modules

Source: Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities, CC BY 3.0. The pinned source,
license and hashes are in `../lie20/source-lock.json` and
`../lie20/LeePerrySmith_License.txt`.

Changes: open orbital reconstruction and native blink from LIE-23; nasal
region removed from the captured base; triangulated nose-free skin cap; shorter
and wider artistic nose variants with a fixed collar and shared boundary;
localized neutral captures, explicit ownership, and rigid capture coordinates.
These variants are derived from one attributed face, not additional scanned
people. The editable Blender master and captures are generated by
`tools/lie24_prepare_blender.py`.
